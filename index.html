The gap between your golden sample and your final delivery

Most quality failures in private label and specialty food manufacturing don't happen at the sample stage. They happen quietly, after approval, as production scales and shortcuts creep in. We provide independent, on-site inspection across the Mekong Valley, operating outside local supplier networks so our findings aren't shaped by a commercial relationship with the factories. What we find is what you get, before it becomes your problem at the port.

Why independent oversight

Quality checks run by parties connected to a supplier's own network often weigh the relationship over the finding. Large firms tend to staff routine audits with rotating local generalists working from standardized checklists. Flying your own team in for every check is expensive and slow. We're a third option: direct access to an inspector with a deep culinary and food production background and no ties to local supplier networks, checking beyond the checklist as well as against it.

What we catch

Spec drift shows up in identifiable places. A cheaper protein swapped in after the reference sample. Moisture content pushed to the edge of tolerance to cut cost. A packaging material changed without notice. Sanitation that holds for the morning of an audit and lapses the week after. We know where the pressure points in a production run sit, and that's where we look first.

Core services

  • Pre-shipment and factory audits: On-site inspection against your specifications, production line checks, documentation review. You receive direct verification that what's being made matches what was approved.
  • Spec drift and quality enforcement: Ongoing or spot-check oversight to catch substitution and corner-cutting. You avoid customer complaints and border rejections before they happen.
  • Technical and regulatory compliance: Assessment aligned to HACCP, ISO 22000, Good Manufacturing Practices, and GFSI-recognized standards including BRCGS and FSSC 22000, plus your own buyer specifications.
