A standard agreement covers money and IP. It leaves open how consistency is tested on the plant floor, how samples are pulled, and what technical thresholds trigger a rejected batch.

  1. I study your golden sample and translate it into measurable chemical and sensory parameters, defined against international standards such as ISO and Codex.

  3. I inspect every run against it, as your eyes and ears on the floor.
  4. When a lot fails, you have documented grounds to reject it, and the factory has already agreed to make it right.
