Product Spec

Stops the product drifting from the one you approved.

Chemical specifications such as pH, °Brix, and water activity, and sensory specifications such as color, flavor, and texture, all set as measurable ranges.

Packaging Specification

Stops quiet material changes and print errors reaching the shelf.

Standards for the pack itself, such as seal and closure, print and labeling, barcode and lot codes, cartons and pallets. I check that pallets are built and wrapped to your Packaging Specification, so product leaves the dock packed correctly for the journey.

Verification Protocol

Stops the first two documents sitting on paper with nobody checking.

How every run is checked, such as sampling plans, on-site measurements, and defect classes, plus what happens when a lot fails.

The reports

A digital report after every visit with photos, video, and readings against your spec. Each lot is tracked by where it lands inside its range, so a plant drifting toward the loose edge is flagged even on a pass. You also get a clear record if a run is ever disputed.

The person on the ground

Your representative at the plant. I log deviations, review ingredient and process changes, and keep one sealed sample from every inspection. When findings are hard, I deliver them constructively, so suppliers fix root causes instead of defending them.
