About us

Jamie Gillen

Principal

I first came to Vietnam to build a manufacturing business, and experienced firsthand the real cost of spec drift and the practices behind it. That process also gave me a real understanding of the local culture and how to work within it effectively. That's the reason this firm exists.

When you work with Mekong Food Integrity and Compliance Associates, you're working directly with me. No layers, no rotating staff, no handoffs.

My background is nearly twenty years in hands-on culinary and hospitality work, spanning five-star hotel chains, Michelin-level restaurants, and private yachting. I served as executive chef, holding ultimate responsibility for standards, sourcing, and output. That foundation includes a diploma from the Culinary Institute of Canada and Red Seal certification.

That career also meant buying and provisioning food across a wide range of countries, building a firsthand understanding of global supply chains, ingredient sourcing, and cold chain handling. Years in private yachting and as a private chef also instilled a deep habit of discretion, the same standard I apply to every client relationship and every finding I report.

I hold certifications in HACCP, ISO 22000, Good Manufacturing Practices, and pre-shipment inspection under ISO 2859, along with GFSI recognized standards including BRCGS.

I bring the same trained eye to your supply chain that I brought to every kitchen I've run: the difference between a dish that looks right and one that actually is, and the discipline to say so plainly when it isn't.
