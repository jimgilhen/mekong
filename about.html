About

Independent, on the floor, and answering only to you.

Why it exists

I built Mekong QA Advisory on one premise: I answer exclusively to you. There are no factory kickbacks and no commission incentives tied to plant throughput. When you hire me, you deal directly with me. No junior handoffs, no middle management, just direct, experienced representation on the factory floor.

What a kitchen taught me

A career in professional kitchens and on private yachts meant accountability for every ingredient, strict cold-chain management, and consistency under pressure. It also instilled a habit of discretion: the standard I bring to every client relationship and every finding I report.

Working with factories

A factory may say “no problem” when it means “we can’t hit that range,” because saying no can cost someone face. So I put every critical range in writing and have the factory initial it. Problems come up on paper before a run, not in front of an audience after it. When findings are hard, I deliver them constructively, so suppliers fix root causes instead of defending them.

Personal

Originally from Nova Scotia, Canada, I spend my time outside plant-floor verification exploring Southeast Asia, sailing, alpine snowboarding, adventure motorcycling, and training in Muay Thai.
