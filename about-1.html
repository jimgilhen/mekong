About

Why Mekong QA Advisory exists

The industry standard in overseas manufacturing is broken. Traditional sourcing brokers chase volume and factory relationships, while conventional auditing agencies send rotating junior staff with generic checklists. I operate entirely differently.

I built Mekong QA Advisory on one premise: I answer exclusively to you. There are no factory kickbacks and no commission incentives tied to plant throughput. I provide an independent, on-the-ground technical defense in Southeast Asia that locks in your specifications, verifies production data, and protects your capital before a container ever leaves the dock.

The culinary and operational pedigree

That standard is backed by nearly twenty years of hands-on leadership in elite professional kitchens, luxury hotels, and private superyachts. I’m a Canadian Red Seal Certified Chef with a diploma from the Culinary Institute of Canada, reinforced by formal technical certifications in HACCP, GMP, and ISO 22000 / FSSC 22000 systems. My career required rigorous accountability for every ingredient, strict cold-chain management, complex global provisioning, and consistency under intense pressure. Years in private yachting also instilled a strict habit of discretion: the standard I bring to every client relationship and every finding I report.

Working with factories

A factory may say “no problem” when it means “we can’t hit that range,” because saying no can cost someone face. So I put every critical range in writing and have the factory initial it. Problems come up on paper before a run, not in front of an audience after it. When findings are hard, I deliver them constructively, so suppliers fix root causes instead of defending them.

Personal

Originally from Nova Scotia, Canada, I spend my time outside plant-floor verification exploring Southeast Asia, sailing, alpine snowboarding, adventure motorcycling, and training in Muay Thai.

When you hire Mekong QA Advisory, you deal directly with me. No junior handoffs, no middle management, and no administrative bloat, just direct, experienced representation on the factory floor.
