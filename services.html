Primary responsibility Owns the recipe, the brand, and the final call on every lot. Facility licensing and food safety operations. The contract and how commercial risk is allocated. Whether the product looks, tastes, and smells the way you approved it. Whether it keeps doing so, batch after batch, and not just on the first run. Recipe & approved product Owns the recipe and approves the reference product that sets the standard. Produces to your recipe and its own process. Protects your recipe and confidentiality in the agreement. Works from your approved finished product, not from your recipe. Compares each production run with that approved product. Food safety & regulatory Engages its own regulatory advisor for destination-market rules, including whether the label meets destination-market law. Owns the food safety plan, environmental monitoring, safety testing, and facility licensing. Writes the factory’s regulatory obligations and indemnities into the agreement. — — Product spec Approves the product spec. Acknowledges the product spec and follows its standard operating procedures. Incorporates the product spec as a binding warranty of product quality within the master agreement. Sets measurable ranges for your approved product (color, pH, Brix, viscosity, water activity, salinity, ABV where applicable, texture, fill weight, taste and smell) from baseline readings. Checks finished product against the spec with my own instruments, so you aren’t relying only on the plant’s own report. Packaging specification Approves it, or hires a packaging consultant to write it. States its packaging specs when I ask for them, if you use the plant’s own packaging. Incorporates the packaging specification into the agreement. Records the packaging standard, from your consultant or the plant’s specs, so it can be inspected: print quality, ingredient list and allergen statement as printed against your approved artwork, barcode, lot codes, cartons, pallets. Checks printing, labels, cartons, and pallets against it on the line. Verification protocol Approves the protocol. Runs its own line measurements. Incorporates the finished protocol into the agreement. Sets sampling plans, on-site test methods, and defect classes. Confirms agreed sampling and test methods are actually being followed on the line, and runs the on-site checks myself.
