Operational Alignment

Mekong QA Responsibilities Co-packer Plant Responsibilities
  • Define sampling plans, defect criteria, and non-negotiable limits.
  • Perform independent verification on finished lots prior to release.
  • Audit batch documentation and root cause analyses for deviations.
  • Escalate critical out-of-spec findings directly to brand leadership.
  • Execute day-to-day floor monitoring and line checks.
  • Maintain operational sanitation, HACCP, and logbook entries.
  • Provide full access to batch records, COAs, and production data.
  • Implement corrective actions for identified operational gaps.

Engagement Models: Retainer vs. On-Demand

Retainer Model

Continuous oversight and embedded support for ongoing production runs, recurring factory audits, and daily quality verification.

On-Demand Model

Targeted pre-shipment inspections, single-run line start-up verification, and rapid defect containment for specific production batches.
